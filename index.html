Questions We Hear

What are the first signs of foundation settlement in Olympia homes?

Common early indicators include diagonal stair-step cracks in exterior concrete or brickwork, interior drywall cracks radiating from door and window frames, sticking doors that fail to latch, and unlevel sloping floors.

Does foundation repair require a building permit in Olympia?

Yes. Structural underpinning, pier driving, and significant wall reinforcement require building permits from the City of Olympia or Thurston County, along with signed structural engineering calculations.

Can foundation repair be done without destroying landscaping?

Yes. Our hydraulic installation techniques utilize compact excavation pits directly against the footing, preserving your yard, flowerbeds, and nearby paved surfaces.
