Why Olympia houses move

Most of Thurston County sits on dense glacial till left by the last ice sheet. On top of it is a layer of sand, silt and, on many lots, fill that was pushed around to make a flat building pad. Houses built partly on fill and partly on till tend to settle unevenly.

Then there is the rain. Around 50 inches a year falls here, most of it between October and April, and the ground stays wet for months. Wet soil pushes on basement walls, washes out fill under slabs and keeps crawl spaces damp.

That is why a good fix often starts with water, and why piers here commonly run somewhere around 12 to 30 feet deep.

Common questions

Do you come out and look before giving a price?

Yes. Foundation problems are hard to price from photos, so we look at the house, including the crawl space or basement and the drainage outside, before we write an estimate.

Do foundation repairs need a permit here?

Most structural foundation work, such as piering or bracing a wall, needs a building permit from the city you live in (Olympia, Lacey or Tumwater) or from Thurston County if you are outside city limits. The permit office may also ask for a design or letter from a licensed engineer. Smaller, non-structural jobs may not need a permit; the permit office makes that call. We confirm what your job needs before we put it on the schedule.

How deep do piers go around Olympia?

Commonly somewhere around 12 to 30 feet. Much of Thurston County has dense glacial till under softer sand, silt or fill. Filled ground near Budd Inlet and the Deschutes tends to be deeper.

Is every crack a foundation problem?

No. Thin vertical cracks in poured concrete are often shrinkage from when it cured. Stair-step cracks, horizontal cracks, doors that stop latching and cracks that keep growing are the ones to have looked at.

Does insurance cover foundation repair?

Usually not when the cause is settlement or soil movement. Sudden events like a burst pipe are a different story. Your insurer is the one to ask.

What does a repair warranty cover?

Terms depend on the repair. The warranty for your job is written into your estimate so you can read it before you decide.
