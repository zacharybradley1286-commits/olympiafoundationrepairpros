How we handle your information

Last updated September 27, 2026

This page explains what we do with the details you give us through this website or over the phone.

What we collect

When you send the inspection form, we receive your name, phone number, town or ZIP code, the kind of problem you picked, any note you add, and the page you sent it from. If you call or email, we get whatever you tell us plus your number or address.

Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) to keep the site running and secure. This site does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use it

Only to answer you: to call back, set up an inspection, prepare an estimate and keep a record of the job if you hire us. We don't sell your details or share them for anyone else's marketing.

Who else handles it

Form messages pass through the service providers that host this site and deliver the messages to us. They process the data on our behalf and only to provide that service.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details for as long as we need them to deal with your request and for normal business records, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@olympiafoundationrepairpros.com or call (360) 209-7814.

Children

This site is for homeowners and is not aimed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this page, the date at the top changes too.
