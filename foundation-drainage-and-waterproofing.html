Comprehensive Exterior & Interior Water Management

When persistent winter storms saturate Olympia soils, thousands of gallons of water build immense hydrostatic pressure against buried basement walls and footing perimeters. Without adequate drainage, water penetrates hairline cracks, deteriorating concrete mortar and flooding living areas. We engineer customized water mitigation systems incorporating deep perforated French drains, dimpled exterior drainage membranes, and dual-pump cast-iron sump basins with battery backup power.
