Crawl Space Encapsulation Cost & Moisture Control in Olympia, WA

By Olympia Foundation Repair Pros Editorial Team

Detailed breakdown of crawl space cleanouts, vapor barrier encapsulation, smart sump pumps, and commercial dehumidification in Western WA.

Quick Answer · Direct Summary

Crawl space encapsulation in Olympia typically costs between $5,500 and $14,000, depending on square footage, debris removal, water management, and insulation condition.

Next Question (How much energy does crawl space encapsulation save?): Sealing and conditioning a crawl space reduces annual home heating and cooling energy expenses by approximately 15% to 20% in the Pacific Northwest.

In Western Washington's cool, humid marine climate, traditional vented dirt crawl spaces represent an outdated building practice that invites moisture problems. Saturated ground continually evaporates gallons of vapor into the sub-floor space, creating an environment where wood-decay fungi thrive and fiberglass insulation falls from joists under the weight of trapped moisture.

Full crawl space encapsulation modernizes this vulnerable area by isolating your home's framing from subterranean dampness. A comprehensive project begins with clearing out soiled insulation, fallen construction debris, and rodent contamination. The soil is then graded flat, and a heavy 20-mil reinforced antimicrobial poly liner is rolled across the dirt floor and sealed permanently to foundation stem walls and center support piers.

🎯 Side Quest: Preventing Crawl Space Mold and Structural Wood Rot in Wet Western WA

Water management and air treatment represent crucial secondary components. In homes subject to high water tables, technicians install an interior perimeter French drain connected to a sealed, airtight sump pump station. Finally, a dedicated, Energy Star commercial dehumidifier is installed to maintain sub-floor relative humidity strictly below 55 percent, preventing wood rot permanently.

For typical Olympia homes ranging from 1,200 to 2,500 square feet, complete encapsulation generally ranges from $5,500 to $14,000. While the upfront investment is significant, property owners reap immediate benefits: warmer interior winter floors, improved indoor air quality throughout the home, complete eradication of mold odors, and enhanced structural protection for expensive timber floor joists.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should crawl space foundation vents be sealed or left open?

Modern building science proves that in humid climates like Western Washington, open vents pull damp outdoor air into cold crawl spaces, triggering condensation. Encapsulation permanently seals these vents to keep moisture out.

How thick should a crawl space vapor barrier be?

Standard 6-mil plastic tears easily underfoot; a true encapsulation requires heavy 12-mil to 20-mil multi-ply reinforced liners that withstand decades of crawling.
