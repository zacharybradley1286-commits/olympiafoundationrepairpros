Concrete Slab Leveling vs Replacement: Polyurethane Foam Lifting in Thurston County

By Olympia Foundation Repair Pros Editorial Team

Discover why high-density polyurethane foam injection (polyjacking) is replacing traditional mudjacking and costly concrete replacement.

Quick Answer · Direct Summary

Polyurethane foam lifting restores sunken driveways and walkways for 50–70% less cost than replacement, with zero heavy demolition and 15-minute cure times.

Next Question (How long does polyurethane concrete leveling last?): High-density polyurethane foam is completely hydrophobic, meaning it will never wash away or degrade, providing a permanent lift as long as sub-base soil remains stable.

Uneven, sunken concrete slabs are a common frustration for Olympia homeowners. When soil settles beneath driveways, front entry porches, patios, and garage aprons, the concrete drops, forming hazardous trip edges and allowing rainwater to pool against foundation footings. Faced with this problem, property owners frequently debate between full concrete replacement and modern polyurethane foam lifting.

Tearing out and pouring fresh concrete is exceptionally expensive, disruptive, and messy. Demolition requires heavy equipment that can damage lawns and sprinkler lines, and new concrete takes nearly a month to reach full compressive strength. Worse yet, pouring heavy new concrete over the exact same uncompacted soil often leads to the new slab sinking again within a few seasons.

🎯 Side Quest: Foundation Drainage Solutions: Stopping Hydrostatic Pressure in Olympia

Modern high-density polyurethane foam lifting—frequently referred to as polyjacking—provides an elegant, permanent alternative. Installers drill tiny 5/8-inch holes (the diameter of a penny) through the sunken slab. Specialized injection guns pump liquid polyurethane chemicals beneath the concrete. As the two-part polymer reacts, it expands rapidly, filling subterranean voids, compacting loose gravel, and gently hydraulic-lifting the concrete slab back to level alignment.

Polyurethane offers dramatic advantages over older traditional mudjacking methods that used heavy cement-and-slurry mixes. Poly foam weighs only 2 to 4 pounds per cubic foot compared to mudjacking slurry's 100+ pounds, meaning it adds virtually no dead weight to weak soils. Furthermore, polyurethane cures in just 15 minutes, allowing homeowners to drive their vehicles across the leveled driveway immediately after the technician finishes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can cracked concrete slabs still be leveled?

Yes, as long as the concrete is not completely shattered into small fragments. Once leveled, we fill and seal cracks with elastomeric joint sealants.

Will polyurethane foam harm garden plants or groundwater?

No. Once cured, high-density structural polyurethane is completely inert, non-toxic, and environmentally safe, posing zero risk to nearby soils or waterways.
