French drains and foundation drainage in Olympia

By Olympia Foundation Repair Pros · September 27, 2026

Short answer

A foundation drain collects water in perforated pipe set in gravel and carries it away before it builds up against the walls. It can go outside the footing or inside the slab.

Long stretches of winter rain soak the soil around a house. Wet soil pushes sideways on basement walls and up under slabs. That pressure forces water through cracks and the joint where the wall meets the floor, and over time it can bow a wall inward.

Check the cheap things first

A lot of wet basements and crawl spaces are roof water problems. Before paying for excavation, check that downspouts carry water 6 to 10 feet away, gutters don't overflow, soil slopes away from the house, patios haven't settled toward it, and sprinklers aren't soaking the foundation.

Exterior drains

The crew digs down to the bottom of the footing, cleans the wall, applies a waterproof coating and a dimpled drainage sheet, and lays perforated pipe in washed rock wrapped in filter fabric. This stops water before it reaches the wall. It costs more and disturbs everything next to the house.

Interior drains

When digging outside isn't practical, a channel is cut around the inside edge of the slab or crawl space, pipe is set in rock, and water runs to a sump pump. This controls water that has already reached the foundation. Many houses end up with both: better roof drainage outside and an interior drain as backup.

Sump pumps

Storms knock out power here, so a battery backup is worth it. Make sure the discharge line carries water well away and can't freeze, the basin has a sealed lid, and there's a check valve and a high-water alarm. More on drainage and waterproofing.

Where should drain water go?

Downhill to daylight on a sloped lot, into an approved storm connection, or to a drywell well away from the house. Check local rules before connecting to anything.

More questions

How long does a French drain last?

A drain built with rigid perforated pipe, clean rock and filter fabric can work for decades. Clogged fabric and crushed pipe are the usual reasons they fail early.

Do I need a battery backup for my sump pump?

If the pump protects a finished space, yes. Winter storms around Thurston County can cut power right when the pump is busiest.
