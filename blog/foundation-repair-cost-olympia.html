How much does foundation repair cost in Olympia, WA?

By Olympia Foundation Repair Pros · September 27, 2026

Short answer

Small jobs like sealing a crack or lifting one slab often land in the low thousands. Steel pier work is usually priced per pier, and whole-house underpinning often runs well into five figures.

Nobody can price a foundation repair from a photo, and a quote you get without anyone looking at the house is a guess. That said, you should know the rough ranges before someone walks your crawl space, so here they are for the Olympia area.

Small repairs

Sealing a leaking basement crack with epoxy or polyurethane, or lifting a single sunken walkway panel, often costs somewhere between about $1,500 and $4,500. Price depends on the length of the crack, how many ports it takes, and how far the crew has to haul equipment.

Steel piers

When a corner or a whole wall has dropped, the repair is usually steel piers under the footing. Piers are priced per pier, commonly in the $1,800 to $2,800 range installed. A typical settled corner takes four to ten piers, so a pier job often totals $9,000 to $25,000, and bigger homes or deeper soil go higher.

What moves the price

  • Depth to firm soil. In much of Thurston County piers reach firm glacial till somewhere around 12 to 30 feet down. Filled ground near Budd Inlet and the Deschutes tends to run deeper.
  • Access. Steep lots, decks and tight side yards can mean digging by hand.
  • Engineering and permits. Most structural foundation work, such as piering or bracing a wall, needs a building permit from the city you live in (Olympia, Lacey or Tumwater) or from Thurston County if you are outside city limits. The permit office may also ask for a design or letter from a licensed engineer. Smaller, non-structural jobs may not need a permit; the permit office makes that call. Those fees belong in the quote.
  • Repairs to finishes. Drywall, tile and trim repair after a lift is usually separate work.

Comparing quotes

Put the quotes side by side and check the same things in each: number of piers, whether the per-pier price changes with depth, whether engineering and permits are included, what gets restored, and what the written warranty covers. Two quotes that look far apart are often quoting different jobs.

If you want a number for your own house, ask us to come look. You can also read how pier work is done.

What does one pier cost?

Installed piers in the South Sound are commonly quoted somewhere around $1,800 to $2,800 each. Depth, access and engineering move that number.

More questions

Does homeowners insurance pay for foundation repair?

Usually not when the cause is settlement, soil movement or poor compaction. Policies are more likely to help when a sudden event, such as a burst pipe, caused the damage. Ask your insurer directly.

Is it safe to wait a few months?

It depends on whether the house is still moving. Mark the ends of a crack with a pencil and the date and check again after a few weeks of rain. If it is growing, or doors are getting worse, get it looked at sooner.
