Guides for Olympia-area homeowners

What cracks mean, what repairs cost, and how the work is done, written for houses on Thurston County ground.

  • How much does foundation repair cost in Olympia, WA?Small jobs like sealing a crack or lifting one slab often land in the low thousands. Steel pier work is usually priced per pier, and whole-house underpinning often runs well into five figures.September 27, 2026
  • Seven signs your Olympia house may be settlingThe signs that matter most are diagonal cracks at door and window corners, doors that stop latching, sloping floors, stair-step cracks in brick or block, and cracks that keep getting wider.September 27, 2026
  • What crawl space encapsulation costs around OlympiaFor a typical 1,200 to 2,500 square foot Olympia house, a full encapsulation often lands somewhere between about $5,500 and $14,000. Drainage, cleanup and access move the number most.September 27, 2026
  • Helical piers or push piers: which fits your house?Push piers use the weight of the house to drive steel pipe down to firm soil, so they suit heavier homes. Helical piers screw into the ground, so they suit lighter structures.September 27, 2026
  • Level the slab or replace it?If the slab is still in one piece and the cause of the sinking is fixed, foam leveling usually costs much less than tearing it out. Badly broken concrete is usually better replaced.September 27, 2026
  • French drains and foundation drainage in OlympiaA foundation drain collects water in perforated pipe set in gravel and carries it away before it builds up against the walls. It can go outside the footing or inside the slab.September 27, 2026
  • Seismic retrofits for older Olympia homesA basic retrofit bolts the wood sill to the concrete foundation and braces short cripple walls with plywood, so the house is less likely to slide or tip off its foundation.September 27, 2026
  • Stopping mold and wood rot in a Western Washington crawl spaceRot needs wet wood. Keep crawl space humidity down with a sealed liner, water control and a dehumidifier, and repair framing that has already gone soft.September 27, 2026
  • Horizontal or vertical: which foundation cracks to worry aboutThin vertical cracks are often from concrete shrinking as it cured. Stair-step cracks suggest settlement. Horizontal cracks and bowing walls point at soil pushing in and need attention soonest.September 27, 2026
  • Buying or selling a Thurston County house with foundation issuesHouses with foundation problems still sell. An engineer’s report and a documented, permitted repair make financing and closing much easier for both sides.September 27, 2026
