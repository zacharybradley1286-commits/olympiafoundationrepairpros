Buying or selling a Thurston County house with foundation issues

By Olympia Foundation Repair Pros · September 27, 2026

Short answer

Houses with foundation problems still sell. An engineer’s report and a documented, permitted repair make financing and closing much easier for both sides.

A foundation or crawl space problem found during inspection can stall a sale. Sellers worry about a big price cut. Buyers worry about a surprise five-figure repair. Both sides do better with facts.

The seller's side

Most Washington home sellers fill out Form 17, a written statement about the condition of the property. It asks about known foundation problems, settling, water in the crawl space and drainage. Hiding past water problems, or patching cracks so they don't show, can cause legal trouble after closing.

Get the right people in

A general home inspector's report is a starting point. When it flags settlement, water or rot, bring in a foundation contractor and, for anything structural, a structural engineer. A floor elevation survey and a look at the crack pattern tell you whether movement is old or ongoing, and a written scope gives both sides a real number.

Closing with a repair pending

Buyers and sellers often settle it with a credit based on written estimates, or with money held in escrow until the repair is done. When a registered contractor completes the work under permit and an engineer signs off, buyers, sellers and lenders usually have what they need.

Most structural foundation work, such as piering or bracing a wall, needs a building permit from the city you live in (Olympia, Lacey or Tumwater) or from Thurston County if you are outside city limits. The permit office may also ask for a design or letter from a licensed engineer. Smaller, non-structural jobs may not need a permit; the permit office makes that call.

Will lenders finance a house with foundation damage?

It depends on the loan and the damage. Some loans require the repair to be finished, or money held back for it, before closing. Ask your lender early.

More questions

Can a foundation repair warranty transfer to the buyer?

Sometimes. It depends on the written warranty. Some transfer for a fee or only if the transfer is registered within a set time. Read the warranty itself rather than relying on what was said at the sales visit.
