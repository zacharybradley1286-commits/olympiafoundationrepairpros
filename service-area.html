Where we work in Thurston County

We work on homes in Olympia, Lacey, Tumwater and the unincorporated parts of Thurston County around them.

  • West Olympia foundation repairHillside lots, cut-and-fill pads and uphill-wall seepage.
  • Cooper Point foundation repairSteep inlet lots, bluff seepage and salt air under the house.
  • Lacey foundation repairFirmer till, but washed-out fill under slabs and damp winter crawl spaces.
  • Tumwater foundation repairDeschutes bluff seepage and light post-and-pier homes near the old town core.

Ground changes from one side of town to the other

The west side and Cooper Point are steep, with cut-and-fill lots and water moving through the hillside. Lacey mostly sits on firmer till, where problems tend to be water and washed-out fill under slabs. Tumwater has river bluffs near the Deschutes and older, lighter houses near the old town core.
