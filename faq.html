Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Foundation Repair in Thurston County.

How long does foundation piering typically take?

Most residential piering projects take between 3 and 5 days, depending on the number of piers and depth to load-bearing strata.

Will foundation repair fix my cracked drywall and sticking doors?

Yes, hydraulically lifting the settled foundation footing back toward its original elevation frequently closes drywall fractures and frees sticking doors.

Do you offer financing options for foundation repair?

Yes, we offer flexible low-interest and zero-down financing options to help homeowners protect their homes without delay.
