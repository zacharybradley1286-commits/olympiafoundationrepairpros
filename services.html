Foundation repairs we do around Olympia

Four kinds of work cover most of what goes wrong under houses in Thurston County. Here is what each one is for.

  • Helical and push piers

    For a corner or wall that has dropped. Steel piers go down to firm soil and carry that part of the house. Push piers for heavier homes, helical piers for lighter ones.

  • Crawl space encapsulation

    For damp, musty crawl spaces. Water control where it collects, a sealed liner over the ground and up the walls, closed vents and a dehumidifier.

  • Concrete slab leveling

    For sunken driveways, walks, patios, steps and garage floors that are still in one piece. Foam goes in through small holes and lifts the slab.

  • Drainage and waterproofing

    For water against the foundation. We start with gutters, downspouts and grade, then footing drains, interior drains or a sump where they are needed.

Not sure which one you need?

That is normal. A sinking corner can be a soil problem or a water problem, and a wet crawl space can come from the roof. Tell us what you are seeing and we will look before recommending anything.

Some related work, like crack injection, wall bracing and seismic bolting, is covered in our homeowner guides. Ask when you call if you need it.
