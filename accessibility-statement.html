Accessibility statement

Last updated September 27, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this site, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, zoom or voice control.

What we have done

  • Pages use real headings, labels on every form field and a skip link to the main content.
  • Text and buttons are sized and colored for readable contrast.
  • The site works without a mouse, and the menu can be opened with the keyboard.
  • Illustrations have text descriptions.

Our target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

Trouble using the site?

If anything on this site is hard to use, call (360) 209-7814 and we will help you by phone, or email info@olympiafoundationrepairpros.com and tell us which page gave you trouble. We will fix what we can.
