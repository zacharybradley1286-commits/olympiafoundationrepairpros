About Olympia Foundation Repair Pros

Olympia Foundation Repair Pros is a dedicated structural stabilization and geotechnical contractor serving homeowners throughout Thurston County and the South Puget Sound.

Our approach

With over two decades of local Pacific Northwest experience, our team was formed to provide honest, engineered foundation repairs tailored to Western Washington's unique soil hydrology. We partner with licensed structural engineers and utilize American-made galvanized steel piering systems to deliver permanent structural peace of mind.

Engineered Precision

Every structural underpinning project is calculated, load-verified, and backed by stamped engineering specifications.

Transferable Warranties

We back our steel piering and encapsulation systems with lifetime transferable warranties to protect home resale value.

Customer Integrity

We provide honest diagnostics and never sell expensive piering systems when minor drainage correction is all that is needed.

Community Grounding

Locally owned and operated in Olympia, we are deeply invested in protecting the historic architectural heritage of our region.
