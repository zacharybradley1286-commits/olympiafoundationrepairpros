Who we are and how we work

We fix foundations, crawl spaces, slabs and drainage on houses in Olympia, Lacey, Tumwater and around Thurston County.

What we do

Most of our work is one of four things: steel piers under settled footings, crawl space encapsulation, foam leveling for sunken slabs, and drainage for water against the foundation. The repairs page explains each one.

How we work

  • We look first. We don't price foundation work from a photo.
  • We check the water. A lot of foundation and crawl space trouble here starts with gutters, downspouts and grade. When those are the fix, we say so.
  • Everything in writing. The estimate lists the scope, the price, what is not included, and whether a permit or engineer is needed.
  • Straight talk about lift. Stopping movement is the goal. How much a house can be lifted back depends on the house, and we tell you that before work starts.

Check any contractor

Washington requires construction contractors to be registered. You can look up any contractor's registration, bond and insurance on the L&I verify page. We encourage it.
