About Olympia Foundation Repair Pros

Olympia Foundation Repair Pros is a premier structural foundation and crawl space stabilization company dedicated to safeguarding homes and residential structures throughout Thurston County and the South Sound.

Our Engineering Standards & Approach

Homes in the Pacific Northwest face distinct geological pressures. With over 50 inches of annual precipitation soaking into variable glacial till, Vashon outwash sands, and saturated lacustrine clays, foundation settlement and moisture damage require engineered, long-term stabilization rather than cosmetic patches.

When you contact Olympia Foundation Repair Pros, our local structural specialists evaluate your home’s specific settlement patterns using calibrated laser elevation surveys. We inspect exterior masonry, interior drywall cracks, crawl space post-and-pier supports, and moisture levels to diagnose the root cause of movement.

Permanent Structural Stabilization

Our structural crews install heavy-gauge galvanized steel push and helical pier systems driven deep into stable, load-bearing strata. Every pier is advanced to engineer-verified refusal pressure to halt settlement and provide permanent foundation support.

Data-Driven Surveys

Precision manometer floor surveys map structural deflection across your entire footprint within 1/10th of an inch before work begins.

Evaluated Steel Pier Systems

We install galvanized steel pier systems carrying independent ICC-ES evaluation reports, built to withstand seismic and soil load demands.

Permits & Code Compliance

All structural underpinning is engineered to comply with City of Olympia and Thurston County building codes, ensuring full documentation at resale.

Transferable Warranties

Our structural piering and encapsulation systems include clear, written, transferable warranties to protect your home's long-term value.
