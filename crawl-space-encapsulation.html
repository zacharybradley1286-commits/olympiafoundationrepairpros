Dry, Clean, Energy-Efficient Crawl Spaces in Olympia

Over fifty percent of the air circulating inside your home rises directly from your crawl space via the natural chimney stack effect. In Olympia's damp climate, unsealed dirt crawl spaces harbor standing groundwater, toxic mold colonies, and wood-destroying rot fungi. We install heavy 20-mil fiber-reinforced vapor barriers sealed to the foundation walls, interior perimeter French drains, commercial dehumidifiers, and sealed access doors to transform wet dirt trenches into clean, dry conditioned spaces.
